# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-05

First public release for Microsoft Edge and Chrome.

- **Focus View:** distraction-free article reader (Mozilla Readability + DOMPurify), themes, Lexend / Atkinson Hyperlegible Next / Arial / serif, spacing and width controls, optional glass effects
- **Chunk mode** (on by default): one piece at a time, Space / Shift+Space
- **Drift Guard:** welcome-back recap after tab switches or idle time, skim nudges, three sensitivity presets
- **Anchor marker and resume:** last paragraph actually read, per-page resume (stored hashed)
- **Read Aloud:** sentence and word highlighting, auto-follow, click-to-jump, speed and voice, skips code
- **Focus band:** 1/3/5 lines, reading line or mouse; also on non-article pages
- **Progress and time left** using your learned reading speed
- **Settings page, welcome page, customizable shortcuts** (Alt+Shift+D/S/F/K)
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA (axe), focus trap, reduced-motion and reduced-transparency support, RTL articles
- **Security and privacy:** least-privilege permissions, closed shadow root, trusted-events only, validated messaging, no web-accessible resources, strict CSP, release-build guard
